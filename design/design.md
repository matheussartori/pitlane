# Design: Pitlane

The locked system. Every UI change reads this first; surfaces share it. Change it on purpose; the file is the rule.
Live specimen: `design/index.html`.

## System
- Genre: modern-minimal, app scope (a tool people work in, not a page they read)
- Theme: custom. Idea: "a pit wall: every car in view, only the ones that need you light up"
- Axes: dark/grotesk-sans/neutral. Posture: dense and quiet, a desktop tool in the line of Warp and Linear, never a page. Modes: dark (default) + light (re-derived, never inverted)
- Revised 20 nov 2026 after the person found the first take childish: everything smaller, no pills, no orange fills. Size never signals importance; a small mark does

## Tokens
`design/tokens.css` is the source of truth. Roles:
- Frame: `--color-frame` around the content panel. Ground: `--color-paper` (near-black graphite, hue 255, low chroma), `-2` panels and cards, `-3` hover and menus, `-sunk` terminal, diff, inputs
- Layers alternate, as in Warp (revised 22 nov 2026): neutral graphite with near-zero chroma, never one flat block. Dark: frame near-black, panel graphite, wells, terminals and inputs sunk back to near-black, cards and menus a step lighter. Light: frame grey, panel white, wells grey, cards white again. Adjacent surfaces always differ by one step
- Terminal and agent text use `--color-term-ink` (a step under UI ink, about #dededd) on `--color-term-paper` (the sunk paper in the dark theme, pure white in the light one, Warp's light ground), in Warp's own measure: Hack 13 (`DEFAULT_FONT`, yours to change with ⌘+ and ⌘−) on rows of about 15.6px, which is the face's own line in the terminal (xterm `lineHeight: 1`) and line-height 1.2 in the agent log (revised 24 nov 2026, from 14 on 1.35 and 1.5, after reading both side by side with Warp). The agent log is drawn as a terminal draws Claude Code: one empty row (`1lh`) between entries, an entry's lines on consecutive rows, tool names and what you said in bold 700, the agent's prose in the terminal's face filling the row (no measure, no balanced lines), with `code` and links in `--color-term-code` (Claude Code's lavender), tool arguments in the terminal's ink, what a tool printed one step under it (`--color-ink-2`) and what only counts or notes (lines read, results found, `… +N lines`, your decisions) dimmed to `--color-muted`, as Claude Code dims them, a call or a result too long for its row going on indented under its own text (two cells for a call, under the words and not the ⎿ for a result), an edit as full pastel rows (`--color-add`, `--color-del`) in the file's syntax colours at the log's own size with no box, and selection in `--color-term-select`: calm to read for hours
- Text is drawn with macOS's own smoothing (`-webkit-font-smoothing: subpixel-antialiased`), never `antialiased`: fuller strokes with soft edges, as Warp and native apps draw them (revised 23 nov 2026, after the thin strokes read faint). Reading surfaces in mono (the terminal, the agent log, diffs) add `--text-soft`, a half-pixel bloom of the text's own colour: Warp's soft, full strokes. Never on UI labels
- Alignment: controls in one row share one height (26, or 28 inside the Ask bar) and one font size; a compact 22px control only sits beside a label
- Ink: `--color-ink`, `--color-ink-2`, `--color-muted`; rules `--color-rule`, `--color-rule-strong`
- Accent `--color-accent` (signal orange, hue 58) means "your move" only: the one primary button, focus, and small marks (a 2px stripe, an icon and its word, a lane number). Never a fill behind a row or a card. Under 2% of a screen
- States: `--color-run`, `--color-done`, `--color-fail` (+ `-wash`). Never colour alone: icon + word + colour
- Faces: Geist (variable) for everything, 430 in the UI (`--weight-regular`), 540 for emphasis and 640 only for titles (revised 23 nov 2026: a touch fuller, easier to read); Hack (Warp's face, bundled) is the one mono, for code, terminal, agent log, diffs, branch names, lane numbers and figures that compare (tabular-nums), with Geist Mono only as its fallback. Hack has two weights: bold mono is written as 700
- Scale (revised 23 nov 2026, half a pixel up everywhere and line-height 1.5 in the UI, for reading comfort): 14.5px UI, 13.5 secondary, 12.5 meta; prose 15.5; titles 17 (sheets) and 19 (screens); nothing above 20. Ink is a step stronger than before (secondary text and muted meta both read without effort); icons 16, small 14. Space: 4px grid (`--space-*`), rows 32 to 40, controls 28 (32 prominent). Dark grounds are charcoal (frame L 16.5%, panel 22%), never near-black. Terminal: Hack 14 (Warp's face), bundled. Radius 3/5/7, no pills (round only for switches and meters)
- Depth on dark is lightness + inset hairline (`--ring`), never drop shadow

## Brand
- The mark (23 nov 2026; the flag over a rule went): a P drawn as a track, the straight in ink and the pit lane leaving it and rejoining in ink-2, the car in the pit as a signal-orange dot on the lane. It reads as Pitlane and as a branch landing on main. App icon: the mark on a graphite macOS squircle (top light, hairline rim, soft shadow), `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/` made with `tauri icon` from a 1024 render; the menu bar takes it as a template image; in the UI it is the `mark` icon

## Controls
- Primary: accent fill, accent-ink text, radius 5, height 26 (30 when prominent). One per view
- Secondary: paper-3 fill + hairline ring, same radius and height. Ghost: text only. Destructive: secondary with fail text, always undoable
- Every interactive control ships 8 states: default, hover, focus-visible (instant, never animated), active (scale .97), disabled (.55), loading, error, success
- Inputs sit in `paper-sunk` with a ring; focus is an outline, never a border change

## Product patterns (see the specimen)
- Shell: no title bar. A frame (`--color-frame`, one step darker than the panel) holds a 38px drag strip on top, where the traffic lights sit, and a 56px rail at the side: the repository as a 32px tile (its first letters; the menu of repositories opens beside it), the screens as 20px outline icons in 38px targets, in `--color-ink-2` (the current one in ink on a 12% wash with a slightly heavier stroke), the ones you work in first and the records after a rule (revised 22 nov 2026): Inbox, Board, Lanes, Terminal, Code, Preview; a rule; Ledger, Reports, Performance, on ⌥1 to ⌥9 with a count badge when something waits (its figure in accent on the accent wash when it waits on you, never a solid orange fill: calmer, as Linear's 2026 refresh dims its nav), and at the foot the Claude quota as two thin bars and Settings. Hover names the screen and its key beside the rail. Everything else sits in one inset panel: 8px from the frame, 10px radius, a hairline. Research: docs/research/ui-trends.md
- The Tower (signature): folded away by default (revised 22 nov 2026); a Tower mark at the rail's foot, above Settings, and ⌘\ open and close it, and the choice is remembered. Folded, the rail's Lanes count still says when a lane needs you. Open, it is a compact list like a terminal's tab list. Each row: the number in small mono, the branch, a mono sub-line (idle days, dirty files, ahead/behind). A row says nothing at the right unless it matters: "You" with its icon in accent, a run's clock, Failed, Done. No row is ever filled with colour. Never reorders on activity
- State marks (`.chip`): an icon and a word in the state's colour, no fill, no outline
- Board groups by who acts next (Needs you, Backlog, Queued, In progress, Landed; Queued added 23 nov 2026: the Backlog holds ideas no run touches, Queued is what Run all works); "By stage" is the alternate lens. Cards that need you carry a 2px accent stripe on the left and their mark in accent, never a wash or a full ring. A card linked to a lane carries the track at card size (8px stops) with the one stop that matters in words (next: main, mixed in dev, 1↓)
- Board columns sit open on the panel (revised 22 nov 2026: wells inside a panel felt cramped): no fill and no ring, 24px between them, a hairline under each head (stronger once the column scrolls). Cards are the only filled shapes: paper-2, radius 7, no ring at rest, ring on hover. Each head is an icon, the name and the count; the Needs you icon and count turn accent when it holds cards. A card whose broken state waits on you (mixed, a failing check) takes the stripe in fail instead of accent. Landed cards step back: title in ink-2, regular weight
- Run all (revised 23 nov 2026): the Backlog head carries Analyse N (search mark), Queued's head carries the run: Start (N) moves the analysed Backlog (held cards aside) into Queued and starts, Continue when Queued already holds cards, Stop (N queued) and Finish now while it works; quiet text with a play mark in accent (never a second primary). Queued is not your move: the queued icon, no accent. Held cards stay in the Backlog with a pause mark and On hold. It runs a session: Analysing (a read-only Claude session per card writes notes and questions on it, once per card), then "Before the run" asks the open questions one at a time with the card as context (Answer later lets the rest start), then starts what can start, top of the Backlog first, and keeps starting the rest as work lands until the queue is empty. While it works the action reads "Stop (N queued)". A paid option stops it and asks once
- Planner on the Board: silent until Run all. Then a queued card the planner holds back carries one muted line in place of its why, naming the other card by number and title ("After #6 Invite limits: both touch invites/index.ts", "Waits for feat/x to land: both touch cookie.ts"). A prediction, never a lock
- Card order: drag a card up or down its column, or to another column the rules allow (it lifts in paper-3 and follows the pointer, its place stays faded, a 2px accent line marks where it lands; a column that cannot take it shows the not-allowed cursor and a muted head, no wash), or Alt+arrows on a focused card (left and right change column). Moves follow one table (`cards::move_rule`), the same for the drag, the card sheet's Move to… (a .pop of valid destinations, each with its column icon) and MCP; running, checking, review and landing are Pitlane's to set. A running card moved out asks first and stops, keeping its session. The order is the board's (`rank` in the card file) and Run all follows it. A card's number leads its top line in ink-2; a note count sits at the right
- Card sheet: wide (1080px, 88vh). Head: number, state mark, where it sits, and the title as a borderless field in the sheet title face. Body: the main column holds the Spec (prose 14 in Geist, grows with its text), open questions, and Notes; a 300px side column on paper holds State, Lane with its track, Runs, Decisions. Notes are a thread on a hairline: who (Claude, You, Judge) and when in muted 11, then the text as small markdown (paragraphs, bullets, `code`, fenced code). Claude adds them through the run's note tool, the analysis and each handoff; you add them under the thread (⌘↩). Every run reads them in its briefing
- Inbox (revised 23 nov 2026): one thing at a time, full column width: a permission an agent waits on first, then questions. The item is a block (paper-2, radius 7, ring): the card it is from, the question at 18 in the title face, its context at 15, the options as 44px rows, your own words, Talk it over, and that card's decisions so far folded. The head carries ‹ n of N › (J/K or arrows); under the block, one quiet Up next line and the keys. Answering moves to the next. Nothing waiting: a calm head (Nothing waits on you, what works and what is queued), Working now, Decided, not started (each card folded), Decided lately; the MCP command only when `claude mcp get pitlane` says it is missing. Primary buttons carry no key inside; the key is in their tooltip
- Inbox items say why they wait, offer options with one recommended, answer with a single key, and record a decision. Questions group under one head per card, with that card's decisions so far as context. Each card is a block (paper-2, radius 7, ring) with its head (number, title, a count with its mark); options are rows with a ring, the recommended one's key and word in accent (revised 22 nov 2026)
- A decision is a block of its own wherever it shows (Inbox, card sheet): the question in ink-2, the answer in ink with a done tick, then the record (D-n) and its actions (Talk, Change) as small 22px buttons with icons. Never a row that squeezes the answer between the tag and the actions
- Diff first: per-hunk Keep and Drop while the card waits on your review (Keep is a quiet mark that you decided; Drop, in the fail colour, reverts that change in the lane's worktree as a commit "review: drop a change in <file>" and refuses if the lane moved), a Viewed tick per file, scope this round or the whole card, Open sends the file to Code
- Lanes screen: one table grouped by who acts next (Waiting on you, In progress, Ready to release, Shipped, Parked). Waiting on you is only what is stuck without you (conflict, mixed with dev, a pull to settle, a review); a lane in dev and not yet in main is Ready to release, calm, with Ship to main as quiet text. Each lane carries the track, four stops (local, remote, integration, production) joined in ink where reached; only the stop your move reaches is accent, a count to settle is an ink ring with its figure, a remote deletion is a dashed stop, contamination is fail. One Next line per lane says the action or the proof (Squashed into main). On this screen the docked Tower folds away: the screen is the Tower at full width
- Run screen: the log as Claude Code shows it (revised 22 nov 2026: the agent pane's lines, ● call(args), ⎿ its result, an edit as red and green lines), paths relative to the worktree. Diffs everywhere (the log, the diff viewer, Review) read as Claude Code's do in Warp (revised 23 nov 2026): pastel rows (`--color-add`, `--color-del`), the line number and the sign in done and fail, bold, and the code in term ink with a light syntax colour (`.tok-*`: keywords violet, strings green, numbers teal, calls blue, comments muted; `src/highlight.tsx`, one line at a time, plain for unknown files and past 4000 lines). The side column has two tabs, This run and Card #n (the spec clamped with More, the decisions, the notes, Open card), so a started card still shows what it was asked; it never scrolls sideways. What changed lists each file with its mark (A, M, D, R in a small square), its name (struck through when deleted), folder muted under it and +added −removed in done and fail, totals in the head. A file opens the diff viewer: a full sheet with the files at the left and the file side by side (before, after, line numbers, removed lines on del, added on add, an empty side hatched) or inline, ↑↓ between files, Open in Code. The head: a back button (secondary, in the panel, never pushed into the margin), the clock ticking in mono in the head beside the state chip, the lane's track under the title, and beside the log the side column in the order you ask (revised 22 nov 2026): the outcome in one line (state, checks pass or fail, N files changed), What changed (each file a row, name in ink and folder muted, opens in Code), How it was checked (each command with its mark, on paper-sunk), the Summary clamped to four lines with More, Next steps, then folded away Where Pitlane pointed it (the brief, files it changed marked), and last the figures on one quiet mono line (turns, cost, tokens in, out), tools that were not allowed with the flow.toml fix. The log is the agent console (below). Ledger (revised 23 nov 2026): four figures on top (Today, Last 7 days, All runs with agent time and tokens, Working now), Where it went (the five cards that cost most, a bar each), then the runs grouped by day with the day's total; each run is the card, its kind and model as people say them (Opus 5.5), the result, the hour, time, tokens and cost. Accounts: one block per login (name, kind, plan, email and organization, signed in), every window its runs reported as a 6px bar with % used and when it resets, then extra usage and the login's renewal; the figures are what Claude Code reported in the account's last run. Quota meters in the top bar only show figures an agent reported
- Review screen: the verdict in one sentence under the title (the Board's words), the primary action follows the evidence (Ask for changes when a check fails or the judge asks; Approve when all passes), the diff per file with a Viewed tick, judge findings at the line they concern (blockers and majors with a fail stripe, the rest quiet), a side column with checks ("1 of 2 pass") and the judge as a compact index of findings, the ask-for-changes composer under them saying which round it sends. While the card waits on your review a line's number is a target (a + in accent on hover, as on GitHub): a click opens a comment under that line (⌘↩ saves, Esc lets go); a saved comment sits under its line on paper-2 with a 2px accent stripe, its number turns accent, and it goes with the next round as file:line, with the findings you tick (the composer says how many). Comments are kept per card until sent
- Merge queue: shipping always goes through the queue. A conflict or failure stands only while it is the newest try of that branch into that target in that repository; a later try that went in closes it (23 nov 2026, after a lane stayed in Waiting on you for a conflict already resolved). Row actions in Lanes are muted until the row is hovered, focused or selected, and keep their gap. A lane in line is in progress, not waiting on you; its Next cell says "2nd in line for dev" or "Shipping to main" with a spinner. A conflict the queue hit puts the lane back in Waiting on you with the file and a Clear action. The Lanes summary adds one clause ("Shipping feat/x to main, 1 more in line."); Ready to release offers "Queue all N for main" when more than one lane is ready for production
- Dictate: a quiet "Dictate" with a mic beside a long field's label (a card's Spec). Click, speak, click again; listening shows the mic in accent, a 28px ink meter and the clock in mono; Writing with a spinner while Whisper runs on this Mac (the bundled large-v3-turbo); the words land where the cursor was. Esc cancels. Silence writes nothing and says how to allow the microphone
- Preview (⌥6): a lane's dev server inside Pitlane. A 44px bar: the address as a search field in mono, Reload, Open in the browser, then the servers the terminals printed (Vite's "Local: http://localhost:5173/" and the like; the newest first, each with the terminal it came from, the chosen one with a hairline ring). The page is a native view over the screen's area, only this machine's addresses (links elsewhere open in the browser); it steps aside under a sheet or ⌘K and on other screens, and keeps its state. Nothing chosen: the newest server; none printed: what to do and ⌘J (added 23 nov 2026)
- Terminal where you are (⌘J, from any screen): a run or a card in review opens its worktree, an open card its lane's, Lanes the selected lane's, Code the folder it shows, anywhere else the repository; a tab already open there (a shell still running) is chosen over a new one; on the Terminal ⌘J goes back where you came from (added 23 nov 2026)
- Go to anything (⌘K, or ⌘P as in Warp; on the Terminal ⌘K clears, so ⌘P): a 640px dialog 12vh from the top, paper-2, radius 10, a 48px field and rows of 34px (icon, title in ink, a muted second part, the key at the right in mono). Empty, it lists Actions (each with its key, so it teaches them), Agents at work (waiting on you first), open Cards (Needs you first, with the Board's label) and the screens; typing also reaches lanes and landed cards. Enter does the obvious thing (open the card, the run, the lane; a card in review opens its review); ⌘Enter the second, named in the foot (a card's last run, a terminal in a lane's worktree, an agent in Terminal › Agents). The strip carries its command center: a quiet field centred on the window, "Search <repo>" and ⌘K (added 23 nov 2026)
- Search field (`.search-field`): a quiet paper-2 fill with the search icon inside, no ring at rest, a hairline on hover, the focus outline when typing; the browser's own search chrome removed. Every in-app search uses it
- Code tab: VS Code (code-server, bundled) fills the panel and wears the design system, as Cursor reshapes VS Code: flat (no modern-UI floating panels), the activity bar as Pitlane's rail (18px icons in 36px rounded targets, no side marker), sentence-case headers, Geist and Geist Mono, sidebar rows with rounded inset selection, tabs as small rounded chips, a quiet status bar, popovers with 8px radius. The folder picker (repository or a lane's worktree) sits in VS Code's own side bar title. Its corners take the panel's 10px radius over the frame colour. Ask Claude about this (⌘L, or the editor's menu; a bundled extension, apps/desktop/vscode-bridge) asks in VS Code's own input box what should change, and the selection (or the cursor's line) becomes a Backlog card: the ask, the file and lines, the lane or repository, the code in a fence; a toast opens it (added 23 nov 2026). Pitlane's colours are written into Dark Modern and Light Modern. The look goes in past VS Code's CSP as a constructed stylesheet and FontFace bytes (apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/vscode-look.css, vscode-look.js, vscode-place.js)
- Terminal screen: your own login shell in a real PTY, in the repository or any lane's worktree. Tabs at the side by default (240px, like Code's tree; on top as an option), each tab a tree of panes. A tab row: the place in small mono (a name you give it wins; the program's OSC title is only the second line, never the name), the folder under it, and at the right only what matters, in icon and word: Needs you and Waiting in accent text, Working muted, Exited in fail. Tabs never move by themselves. Panes split with ⌘D / ⌘⇧D and, as in Warp, the panes along that split share the room equally; each with a 28px bar (place, title, zoom, close) only when the tab has more than one; the focused bar carries a 1px accent underline and the others' text dims. A divider is a 1px rule with an 8px hit area; drag to resize, double-click to share the room equally. Drag a pane by its bar onto another pane's side (a half-pane wash in accent at 9%), its middle to swap, or onto a tab. Pane body: 12px top, 16px left, Hack 14 by default (Warp's face; Menlo, SF Mono and Geist Mono stay in Settings) at line-height 1.35 in term-ink on paper-sunk, drawn by WebKit with macOS's smoothing (revised 23 nov 2026; Sharp (GPU) stays in Settings for very heavy output), a 2px bar cursor; the 16 colours are the `--ansi-*` tokens, every program's colour lifted to 4.5:1. Command blocks, as in Warp (zsh marks commands with OSC 133 through Pitlane's startup files, which source yours first): a hairline above each command, a failed one with a 2px fail stripe in the gutter and a 7% fail tint, on hover its time (exit code when it failed) and Copy command, Copy output, and on a failed one Make a card to fix this (the command, the lane or repository it ran in, its exit code and the last 80 lines of output go on a new Backlog card; a toast opens it); ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ select the previous or next block so ⌘C copies it; inside a long block its command stays at the top of the pane on paper-2 with its clock, a click goes back to it. Find (⌘F) floats top right in the pane. The empty state is centred: a head, one line, the places as a list, the three keys. The Ghostty key set (docs/research/terminals.md). The layout is kept and comes back after a restart with fresh shells in the same folders
- Agent console (added 23 nov 2026): one Claude Code session reads the same wherever it shows, a card's run and Terminal › Agents (`src/console.tsx`). On paper-sunk: the log in the Terminal's face at its size less 1px, line-height 1.45, the agent's prose in the same face (bold in ink, code in term-code) with a blank line's worth of air between blocks; a call's arguments in ink-2, its result muted; every line keeps one gutter: ● a call (done, fail, muted while it runs), ⎿ its result, · a setup or session note, ? a question asked, ✓ ✕ a check or the end (Finished · turns · cost), what you said with › in accent on paper-2. Paths read relative to the worktree. Scrolled up to read, the log stays put as lines arrive (an open screenshot stays open) and a Jump to present floats over its bottom edge; at the end it follows again (added 23 nov 2026). A question that waits shows at the end of the log (1-4), never outside it; Working… with the spinner when no call is open. A subagent's Task opens its steps in place, with a crumb back. Under the log, one hairline, the prompt (›, Enter sends, / for commands, Ctrl+C stops) and the status line (model, effort, account). In a page it is a well (radius 7, ring); in the Terminal it fills the pane under its 40px bar
- Lanes proves merge state (ancestry, squash patch-id, PR) and never offers to delete a live or locked worktree
- Every data region has loading (skeleton in the shape of the content), empty (what goes here + one action), error (what broke + what to do), full

## Motion
- Character: brisk and still. 120ms press and colour, 200ms hover and menus, 360ms panels and meters
- transform and opacity only. No pulsing dots: a running agent shows its clock ticking in mono. The only loops are the spinner and the skeleton

## Voice
- English and Brazilian Portuguese UI: English strings are the keys (`t()` in apps/desktop/src/i18n.ts, translations in locales/pt). Say what happened, why, and the next step, in the user's nouns
- Never "Oops", exclamation marks, or announcing the AI. Never a generic "waiting for input"

`design/tokens.css`, `design/base.css`, `design/components.css`. Tailwind or DTCG blocks get added here on request.
